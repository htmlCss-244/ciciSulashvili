History of Tbilisi

Tbilisi, the capital of Georgia, was founded in the 5th century by King Vakhtang Gorgasali. According to the legend, while hunting, the King's falcon caught a pheasant, which fell into a hot spring and was healed. Fascinated by this fact, the King ordered the construction of a city, which was named Tbilisi due to its "warm" waters. Throughout the centuries, Tbilisi has been at the crossroads of many cultures, which is reflected in its architecture and character. Today, it remains one of the most important cultural, economic and political centers of the Caucasus region.
